How it works

Three steps from email to sign-in.

01

02

Email matching runs in your browser; OtpPilot has no email-processing server.

Privacy details

OtpPilot uses Google’s Gmail read-only permission for the connected account. That permission can read any message in the account; OtpPilot’s search is limited to up to five recent messages matching your rules. A dedicated inbox keeps verification mail separate.

Demo uses sample values. It does not connect to Gmail or make network requests.
